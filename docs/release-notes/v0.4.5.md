## Sentry 0.4.5

Two fixes from watching the alerts over a full-screen flight app. When Sentry alerts, how often, and which sound it plays have not changed.

**Every pop-up is now a dark card.** 0.4.4 fixed the traffic banners, but the other pop-ups still used Android's standard white notification: "Bound aircraft lost", "Bound aircraft back", "Internet offline" / "Internet back", "Sentry restarted", "Sentry sounds on" and the pre-flight result. The status notification in the shade (Disarm · Open Sentry) and the "new version available" notice did too. All of them now use the same dark card as the traffic banners, with their own colours whatever the controller's theme:
- amber when something is lost (drone position, internet, a failed pre-flight);
- green when it is back (or the pre-flight passed);
- grey-blue for everything else.
The pop-up is the title and one line, about the same height as a COLLISION RISK pop-up. Pull the shade down to read the whole message. The status notification's Disarm and Open Sentry buttons are now inside its card.

**The "Clear: move …" hint no longer flips.** In the demo encounter, the WARNING pop-up said "Clear: move NW", the banner a few seconds later said "move SE", and then it said "move NW" again. The aircraft hadn't passed. The hint picked the side from where your drone was *at that moment* compared with the aircraft's path, and for one second the drone sat almost exactly on the aircraft's path line. Sentry then used a fallback and chose the wrong side. The hint now uses where your drone **will be when the aircraft passes closest**, which takes the drone's own movement into account. In the demo encounter that is NW every second from the first alert to the pass. The hint also no longer changes on a single reading: a new direction has to hold for 5 seconds, unless the aircraft turns more than 45°, which shows the new direction at once.
