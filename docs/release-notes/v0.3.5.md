## Sentry 0.3.5

**Sentry now has its own voice.** The DJI RC Plus has no text-to-speech engine, so until now it showed "Voice unavailable" and played only tones. Sentry now includes a recorded voice that works with no text-to-speech and no Google services. If the controller has a working text-to-speech engine, Sentry uses it. If that engine is missing or fails on a callout, Sentry switches to its own voice straight away for that callout and the ones after it. It tries the engine again five minutes later.

The main screen's **Voice** row shows which voice is speaking: "OK (bundled voice)" or "OK (Google TTS)". **Settings → Voice → Test voice** plays a full warning through that voice.

**Callouts now repeat based on range and closure.** The closer and faster an aircraft is closing, the more often Sentry repeats the callout:

| Aircraft | Repeated |
|---|---|
| more than 3 nm away, or moving away | not repeated. You hear it once, again if it gets more serious, and "clear" when it leaves |
| 1–3 nm, closing (caution or warning) | every 20 s |
| 1–3 nm, advisory | every 30 s |
| 0.5–1 nm, closing | every 12 s |
| inside 0.5 nm, or on course to pass within 0.5 nm in 30 s or less | every 6 s, short: "Traffic, N388KM, west, 1,500 feet, 200 below, closing." |
| moving away after a close pass | "N388KM passing, diverging." once, every 45 s while it is within 3 nm, then "clear" |

A callout that gets more serious is still spoken at once. Only one callout per aircraft waits in the queue. With two aircraft at the same level, the closer one is called first.
