## Sentry 0.3.3

**Buttons always reachable.** On the real RC Plus the ARM/DISARM, Test and Settings buttons could be pushed off the screen with no way to scroll to them. They now sit in a bar pinned to the bottom, and every panel above it scrolls. Sentry is now laid out for the controller's real screen, which the emulator reproduces at 400 dpi.

**One aircraft per controller.** Pin this controller's aircraft by serial in Settings: type it, or tap it in the list of aircraft in the feed now. Sentry protects **that aircraft only** and never switches to another drone. When it isn't in the feed, the banner reads "WAITING FOR THIS CONTROLLER'S AIRCRAFT", Sentry says so once, and the controller cylinders protect you until it appears. The main screen always shows "Bound to: <serial> · <callsign>". The callsign pattern, serial list and Drone… picker are gone.

**Nothing under the drone is ignored.** The protected space now runs from the ground up to 2,000 ft above the drone (Settings: "Ceiling above aircraft"). Traffic below the drone always counts.

**Cleaner Targets list.** It shows traffic within 10 nm of your aircraft (15 nm of the controller when the aircraft isn't in the feed), and hides traffic above 18,000 ft. All three are settings. Any aircraft Sentry is warning about is always shown.

**Clear feed status.** The Drone feed line says why it's empty: no token, token rejected, feed error, or no drones in the feed.

**Stays out of DroneSense's way.** Sentry never comes to the front by itself. Updates never install while armed. Voice ducks DroneSense only while a callout is playing. No prompts appear while armed. A bad feed can't crash it.

Settings now opens with the alert rings, then the controller cylinders, then this controller's aircraft.
