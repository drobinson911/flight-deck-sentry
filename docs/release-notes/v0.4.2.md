## Sentry 0.4.2

**Every alert sounds (Standard style).** Pilots flying in DroneSense, or on an automated mission without the controller in hand, can miss a banner. In the default Standard style every traffic alert now makes a sound: an aircraft entering the 3 mi advisory ring gets one soft tone and a short vibration (its 30 s updates stay silent). Caution, warning and collision risk are unchanged. For the old behaviour, set Settings → Alert style → **Quiet**: advisories are silent there.

**Armed stays armed.** Sentry stays armed until you DISARM it or swipe it away from the app switcher.
- If it crashes or Android stops it, it restarts itself, re-arms and alerts "Sentry restarted".
- If the controller is powered off and on while Sentry is armed, it comes back armed and alerts "Sentry armed after restart". You can turn this off in Settings → Background → Resume armed after power-off (on by default).
- Swiping Sentry away disarms it and shuts it down. It does not restart.

**PASSING stays PASSING.** After a close pass, while the aircraft moves away but is still inside the 0.5 mi ring, the banner used to switch back to "⚠ WARNING · N388KM · Not closing". It now stays "● PASSING · N388KM · Diverging" (grey, silent, updated in place) until the aircraft clears. If the aircraft turns back toward the drone, Sentry alerts again at the right tier.

**Times in words.** Banners, the target list and the log now say "Closest 2,700 ft in 1 min 28 s" and "in 43 s" instead of "1:28".

**Resources.** Sentry now measures what it costs the controller while armed. The main screen's Sources column has a **Resources** row: CPU as a share of the whole controller (and of one core), memory, the battery's drain per hour and Sentry's own network data. **Settings → Resources** shows 1-minute, 5-minute and whole-flight averages, peaks and more. The log gets a summary every 5 minutes and a flight summary at DISARM. The battery figure is the whole controller's drain: Android doesn't give an app its own battery share.

**Share log.** **Settings → Diagnostics → Share log** sends the last 24 hours (every alert, what you tapped, source health, pre-flight results, resource summaries, app and controller details) as a text file through Android's share sheet. **Copy** puts it on the clipboard instead. Drone callsigns, the fleet token and server addresses are removed. The bound serial is kept.

**Overwatch station.** The local ADS-B feed is now called the Overwatch station everywhere: the Sources row, Settings → **Overwatch ADS-B station (URL)**, pre-flight, and the "Overwatch station link lost / regained" alerts.
