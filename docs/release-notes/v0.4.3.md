## Sentry 0.4.3

Fixes from an alert-by-alert check of 0.4.2 on an emulated RC Plus (same screen size and text size as the controller).

**Zone entry banner stays up.** When an aircraft entered a TFR near your drone, Sentry posted "▣ ENTERING TFR …" and then changed it to "△ ADVISORY" a split second later, so you never saw the zone entry. An alert banner now keeps its wording for the whole time it is on screen (5 s by default). The countdown underneath still updates.

**One message when your aircraft comes back.** When the bound aircraft dropped out of the drone feed and then came back, two pop-ups appeared at once ("Bound aircraft acquired" and "Bound aircraft back") and only the second could be read. Now you get one: "Bound aircraft back". "Bound aircraft acquired" appears only the first time your aircraft is found after arming.

**No more cut-off pop-ups.** "Internet offline", "Bound aircraft lost", "Sentry restarted" and "Sentry armed after restart" were cut off with "..". They are now short enough to fit on one line:
- Internet offline: "Cloud traffic, drone feed and TFR updates paused until it is back". If your Overwatch station is connected: "Cloud traffic, drone feed, TFRs paused; Overwatch station still live".
- Bound aircraft lost: "Drone position lost; controller cylinders are the fallback".
- Sentry restarted: "Stopped unexpectedly and restarted itself; armed and watching again".
- Sentry armed after restart: "Re-armed after a controller restart or app update; watching again".

**COLLISION RISK doesn't flicker.** In the crossing replay, COLLISION RISK dropped to WARNING for one second and then went back up. Small altitude steps in the feed made the "climbing through your altitude" test switch on and off. Once COLLISION RISK is up, Sentry now allows the same margin it already allows WARNING and TRACK before dropping it, so a one-second glitch in the prediction can't lower it. The alert still ends normally once the aircraft passes.

**Smaller fixes**
- Settings: the advisory ring text now says what the Standard style does: one short tone and a banner when an aircraft enters, then silent updates. In Quiet, advisories are silent.
- Main screen: the "1 nm" and "0.5 nm" ring labels no longer overlap.
- The status notification says "1 target", not "1 targets".
