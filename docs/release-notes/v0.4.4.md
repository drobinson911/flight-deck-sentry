## Sentry 0.4.4

Two fixes to the traffic banner, from a tester report on the RC Plus: "I couldn't see the black writing in the alert… notification too large." Nothing about when Sentry alerts, how often, or which sound it plays has changed.

**Readable on the controller.** The banner's text used Android's own notification text colour, which is black unless the whole system is in night mode. The RC Plus draws its pop-ups on a dark background without being in night mode, so the text came out black on dark. Every banner is now its own dark card with its own colours, whatever the controller's theme:
- the level and aircraft in colour: COLLISION RISK red, WARNING amber, CAUTION yellow, TRACK and ADVISORY grey-blue, PASSING grey;
- everything else in near-white;
- a coloured edge on the left in the level's colour.
All text is at least 7:1 contrast against the card.

**Smaller pop-up.** The pop-up is now two lines plus the buttons, about a quarter shorter than 0.4.3 (96 dp instead of 133 dp on the RC Plus screen):
- line 1: level, aircraft, and where it is ("⚠ WARNING · N388KM · SW 1.9 mi · 155 kt · ≈100 below");
- line 2: closest approach and time, with the "Clear: move NW" hint on the right;
- buttons: Got it · Ignore · Quiet 5 min.
COLLISION RISK and PASSING have no buttons, so their pop-up is just the two lines. Pull the notification shade down to see the full four-line banner with the buttons.

**The buttons are Sentry's own.** Got it, Ignore and Quiet 5 min now sit inside the banner card instead of the system's button row. They do the same things as before.
