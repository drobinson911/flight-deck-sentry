## Sentry 0.3.2

Fits the RC Plus screen: no more truncated buttons, panels reflow at 320 dpi.

- The RC Plus panel is 1920×1200 at about 320 dpi (about 960×600 dp). Earlier builds were laid out for 1280×800 dp, so the buttons read "AR", "T", "Dr", "Sett" on the controller.
- Below 1000 dp wide: a smaller compass with the drone panel under it, ARM on its own full-width row, Test / Drone… / Settings below it, and smaller type. Button labels shrink to fit and never truncate.
- Settings is one scrolling column on the controller.
- Sources and Targets rows move long details to a second line instead of cutting them. The callouts panel ends in "…" instead of a half-cut line.
- Checked on an Android 10 (API 29) image at the RC Plus's density, and at font scale 1.3.
