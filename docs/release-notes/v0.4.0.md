## Sentry 0.4.0

**Sounds, vibration and banners. No more voice.** Sentry now alerts with one of the controller's own sounds, a vibration and a heads-up banner, then you look at AirSense / ForeFlight. Each level has its own sound; pick any sound from the controller in **Settings → Sounds & vibration** and use **Test** to hear it.

**It predicts.** Every second Sentry works out when each aircraft will be closest to your drone and how close:

| Banner | When (defaults) |
|---|---|
| ▲ **TRACK** | will pass within 1 mi in the next 2 minutes: one sound |
| ⚠ **WARNING** | will pass within 0.5 mi in the next 60 s, or is inside 0.5 mi: sound, repeated faster as it closes (20 / 12 / 6 s) |
| ‼ **COLLISION RISK** | within 500 ft and 300 ft vertically in the next 60 s, or climbing / descending through your altitude close by: alarm every 3 s until it's over |
| ◆ caution / △ advisory | inside 1 mi / 3 mi (advisory is banner only) |

Only aircraft from the ground up to 1,500 ft above your drone count (setting). The rings work with the drone on the pad too. When it passes and opens you get one "passing" sound, then the banner clears.

**The banner** shows who and where ("SW 7.9 mi · 160 kt · 300 below, climbing"), the prediction with a live countdown ("Passing within 0.3 mi in 2:58"), and which way is clear ("Clear: move NW ↑"). It pops up only when things get more serious; it clears itself after 5 s. Buttons: **Got it** (quiet for that aircraft for 60 s), **Ignore** (until it leaves), **Quiet 5 min**. Any mute ends at once if the aircraft gets more serious or turns toward you.

**TFR alerts** now come only for TFRs at your drone (or within 2 mi), not every TFR in range.

**Pre-flight** (bottom bar) checks internet, token, drone feed, your aircraft, GPS, traffic, TFRs, notifications, battery setting, sound and vibration, and tells you how to fix anything red.

Also: an alert when the internet drops or returns; slower polling while your aircraft is on the pad (less battery); the ongoing notification has **Disarm** and **Open Sentry**. Your ceiling setting moves from the old 2,000 ft default to 1,500 ft (a value you chose yourself is kept).
