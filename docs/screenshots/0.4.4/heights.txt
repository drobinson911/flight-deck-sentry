on screen; the traffic row's `contentHeight` in px (visibleType 2 = heads-up, 1 = expanded in the pulled-down shade).
dp = px / 2.5. Heads-up width 1340 px = 536 dp (HeadsUpManagerPhone mTouchableRegion x 290..1630).
